# 04 — Voice-First & Zero-Literacy Interaction Design

## 1. The Design Challenge
Over 30% of female and 15% of male interstate migrant workers are illiterate. An interface requiring reading text instantly excludes a massive portion of the target population.

## 2. Zero-Literacy Principles

### 2.1 Auditory Prompts as the Primary Navigational Surface
- Every UI card, domain selector, and action button speaks its title and instructions aloud in the user's selected native language (L1) upon single-tap or focus.
- A distinctive earcon/audio chime indicates when the microphone is listening, processing, or speaking.

### 2.2 Visual Symbology & High-Contrast Tactile Tokens
- Universal iconography representing physical workplaces (Construction Helmet, Stethoscope, Delivery Bag, Auto Rickshaw).
- 7:1 contrast ratio with a true black OLED background (`#0A0A0C`) and vibrant amber/cyan action accents for visibility under harsh outdoor sunlight.
- Touch target minimum dimension: `56x56 dp` to support rough or calloused fingertips.

### 2.3 Conversational Initiation & Voice Commands
- The entire app can be driven hands-free via local acoustic keyword spotting ("हे सीधेबोल" / "Hey Seedhebol").
