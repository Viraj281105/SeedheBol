# 3-Minute Stage Demo Runbook (Airplane Mode)

> **Golden Rule**: One narrative. No feature list recitation. Airplane mode enabled in full view of the jury.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Pitch Narrative

| Time | Action & Screen Flow | Speaker Narration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 0:15** | Open slide with Ramesh's photograph. | *"This is Ramesh. 34, from Muzaffarpur, Bihar. He arrived in Chennai 4 days ago for a construction job. If he can't speak basic Tamil in 3 weeks, his supervisor demotes him to unskilled day labor."* |
| **0:15 - 0:25** | **Swipe down Android Quick Settings. Tap Airplane Mode ON.** | *"Everything you see from this moment runs 100% locally on this iQOO 15 phone. No internet. No data cost. No server."* |
| **0:25 - 0:45** | Launch Seedhebol. App speaks welcome in Bhojpuri. Tap construction icon. | *"Ramesh cannot read Tamil script. He can barely read Hindi. Seedhebol is 100% voice-navigated."* |
| **0:45 - 1:20** | **Situational Roleplay**: App plays supervisor in Tamil asking for concrete mix ratio. User answers in spoken Tamil. User interrupts persona mid-sentence. | *"Direct L1 to L2 roleplay with real-time barge-in. ASR, dialogue graph, and neural TTS execute in under 800ms."* |
| **1:20 - 1:50** | **Pronunciation Diagnostic**: Deliberately mispronounce retroflex consonant (படம்). | *"The app pinpoints the exact physical error: your tongue hit your teeth instead of curling back, because your native Bhojpuri phonology lacks this Dravidian contrast."* |
| **1:50 - 2:15** | **Camera OCR**: Point camera at printed Tamil hazard sign. | *"Instantly translates printed regional signage and generates a 5-word micro-lesson from unfamiliar vocabulary."* |
| **2:15 - 2:40** | **Ambient Miner**: Open ambient discovery drawer. | *"While we've been talking, the phone ambiently extracted new Tamil words heard in the room. Zero raw audio stored. Fully DPDP compliant."* |
| **2:40 - 3:00** | **Closing**: Swipe down Quick Settings — **still in Airplane Mode**. | *"140 million migrant workers in India. Seedhebol gives them an economic voice."* |
